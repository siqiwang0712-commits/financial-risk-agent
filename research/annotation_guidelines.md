# Decision-grade Annotation Guideline — Draft / NOT VALIDATED

Reviewers work from a point-in-time evidence packet frozen at `as_of_date`; system predictions are hidden. Reviewer 1 and Reviewer 2 independently label evidence spans, claims, relationships, disclosure tension and T+12-month outcome events. Agreement is accepted; disagreement remains excluded until a third adjudicator records a label and reason.

An outcome event must be public, material and dated inside the registered window. Missing information is not a negative label. Restatements filed after the prediction date are outcome evidence, never silently substituted into prediction inputs. Companies are assigned to exactly one development, validation or frozen-test split for all years.

Quality checks reject duplicate observation IDs, company overlap, labels available before/equal to the prediction date, missing source hashes and incomplete review. The historical three-company `public_v1` pilot remains single-reviewer and is not promoted retroactively. For E3, neutral machine Reviewers A and B agreed on 90/90 normalized states; that result is machine-only and remains `NOT_ADJUDICATED` by humans.
