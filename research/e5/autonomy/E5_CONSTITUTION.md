# Structured E5 scientific constitution

Scientific authority is the explicit human protocol and its reconciled machine
config. **Autopilot != ScientificAuthority.** The autopilot orchestrates checks,
prepares candidate design artifacts and reports blockers; it cannot choose a new
scientific objective, approve its own exception or override a failed gate.

1. Success means executing the frozen study correctly, not defeating a comparator.
   Null, negative and blocked results are legitimate; unfavorable results never
   justify changing a protocol.
2. Future E5 outcomes may never select cohort membership/exclusions, features,
   preprocessing, models, Agents, prompts, thresholds, selective or Assurance
   policy, reference design, endpoint, primary estimands, multiplicity, tests,
   meaningful effects, coverage requirements or claim wording.
3. Historical E1–E4/E4-S/E4-R and frozen development evidence remain immutable.
   Missing required evidence means BLOCKED, never approximation, invented data,
   silent substitution or relaxed eligibility.
4. Protocol, prediction and outcome stages must be technically isolated. A frozen
   stage cannot be silently rewritten or regenerated. Outcome access requires a
   complete verified prediction chain and separately approved mount boundary.
   Absence of paths is a necessary check, not proof of OS-level isolation.
5. Amendments are explicit, timestamped and content-addressed, with old/new hashes
   and cohort/prediction/outcome exposure declarations. Scientific changes after
   prediction freeze invalidate the affected confirmatory identity. Earlier
   post-freeze scientific amendments require a new protocol identity and exposure
   classification; they cannot inherit confirmatory status automatically.
6. Claims must fit evidence. Engineering verification is not prospective empirical
   validation. Hash integrity is not semantic truth or issuer authentication.
   Provenance is not independent evidence. Missingness/observability is not
   financial severity. UNCALIBRATED scores are not outcome probabilities.
7. Structured E5 evaluates selective authorization; E5-Narrative evaluates filing
   grounding. Neither study validates the other. TEVV is separate from outcomes.
8. This mandate ends at READY_TO_FREEZE or BLOCKED. No actual protocol freeze,
   cohort enumeration, predictions, future outcome access or E5 validation occurs.

## Amendment rule

Every amendment records ID, UTC timestamp, reason, affected artifacts, old/new
hashes, exposure (cohort visible, predictions existed, outcomes accessible),
scientific impact and confirmatory identity disposition. Exposure flags must be
monotone with the current stage. Endpoint/model/schema/threshold/estimand/policy/
test changes after prediction freeze mean INVALIDATED, regardless of intent or
whether the result was favorable. A new identity and independent approval are
required before any future execution; no fabricated sign-off is permitted.
