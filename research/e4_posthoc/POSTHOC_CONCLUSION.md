# E4 Post-hoc Conclusion

## What E4 established

`ESTABLISHED_E4`: On 674 prespecified, deterministically verified E4-A
observations, B6 showed a positive paired AUROC improvement over B0
(`+0.030`, 95% CI `+0.014` to `+0.048`, Holm-adjusted `p=0.0015`) against the
predefined financial-deterioration endpoint.

## What E4 did not establish

`EXPLORATORY_E4`: H0 over B0 and H0 over A2. Five paired events are inadequate
for confirmatory inference. E4 also did not establish calibrated default
probability, bankruptcy prediction, stronger-LLM limits, full narrative Agent
validity, universal generalisability, or production/regulatory fitness.

## What post-hoc analysis suggests

`POST_HOC`: Verification is strongly associated with prediction-time
characteristics, so selection bias is plausible. Propensity-weighted
sensitivities kept B6−B0 positive, but unstable weights and an unverified MAR
assumption prevent causal reassurance. Agent continuous rankings are sensitive
to batch context even though binary decisions mostly agree. The local Agent's
weak result is compatible with limited model capacity, but no stronger local
model was installed and external GPT-5 packet transfer was blocked by the
execution environment. A later Codex sub-Agent comparator, displayed under
the project-internal codename `ChatGPT5.6 Sol`, completed 150/150 structured
judgments. Its A0/A1/A2 AUROCs were 0.815/0.800/0.738 and its fixed hybrid
AUROC was 0.708, but only 18 cases and five events were verified. The platform
did not expose the sub-Agent's exact model ID, and this retrospective analysis
is insufficiently powered; it does not establish superiority or change E4.
Fusion and calibration diagnostics are descriptive only.

## What requires E5

E5 needs a new untouched time window, a much larger Agent cohort, a preselected
stronger model, prospective schema and batch gates, improved endpoint coverage,
and an independent calibration split. A full-document track additionally needs
a frozen narrative corpus and adjudicated grounding/contradiction labels.

**Final integrity decision: E4 remains VALID within its frozen, narrow claim.**
